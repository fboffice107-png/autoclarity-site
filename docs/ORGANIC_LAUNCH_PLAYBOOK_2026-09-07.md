# Organic local launch playbook — 2026-09-07

This document prepares owner-managed profiles without inventing a location,
license, phone, hours, category, review, or verification result. No listing was
created or claimed during this audit.

## Google Business Profile status

A limited unpersonalized web search for `AutoClarity` plus Las Vegas/PPI terms
surfaced the AutoClarity website and unrelated businesses/products using similar
names, but no confirmed Las Vegas AutoClarity Business Profile. Map inventory
and ownership were not available from an authenticated Google account, so this
is **not** proof that no profile exists. Before creating anything, search Google
Maps while signed in for the brand, real base address, real phone, founder name,
and likely old names. Claim a legitimate existing profile; do not create a
duplicate.

Google permits one service-area profile for a business that travels to customers
and has no customer-facing storefront. The real base is used when Google
requires it and the address should be hidden publicly. A virtual office, mailbox,
or fabricated storefront is not eligible. Use only the real-world name and the
fewest accurate categories:

- [Google representation rules](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en)
- [Google service-area rules](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481?hl=en)

### If Google requests video verification

Record one continuous owner-controlled video that shows, without exposing
sensitive numbers:

1. the real operating area/base context;
2. real tools, equipment, branded materials, and the mobile-work setup;
3. evidence the business exists, such as the issued permit/license or matching
   business record;
4. evidence the owner manages the operation, such as unlocking equipment or
   showing an owner-controlled business system; and
5. consistent `AutoClarity` branding.

Use only the exact evidence Google requests on the offered verification screen.
Never stage a fake job, location, storefront, employee, tool, or document.

## Other legitimate organic listings

1. **Bing Places:** search/claim before adding; use the same name, hidden-address
   service-area model where supported, owner-controlled phone, website and
   hours. Microsoft documents adding, claiming, editing, and verifying a listing
   in [Bing Places support](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/bing/add-and-manage-your-business-listing).
2. **Apple Business Connect / Maps:** search for the brand first and claim only
   if Apple's business model can represent the service without publishing an
   inappropriate address. Apple's current owner workflow is described in
   [Apple Business support](https://support.apple.com/en-ca/guide/business/axm7909096bf/1/web/1).
3. **Yelp:** claim only a real existing/eligible page with identical identity
   facts. Do not ask customers for Yelp reviews; follow Yelp's no-solicitation
   policy. Do not create a page merely for a citation.
4. **Established owned profiles:** update only profiles the owner already controls
   and only when they can use the same legitimate identity. Skip bulk citation
   services and low-quality directories.

## Ethical review workflow

Activate only after the Google profile is verified and the completion workflow
proves a same-request published report exists.

1. Store the official Google review link as an owner-approved configuration
   value; never use a guessed URL.
2. Ask every eligible real completed PPI customer neutrally through the already
   authorized email channel, once, with a stable dedupe key.
3. Offer no incentive and do not ask for a positive rating or suppress unhappy
   customers.
4. Do not automatically contact historical customers without separate owner
   approval and a communication-law/privacy review.
5. Respond without revealing vehicle, inspection, seller, payment, or customer
   details.

Prepared neutral copy: “Thanks for choosing AutoClarity. If you'd like to share
your experience, you can leave an honest Google review here: [official link].”

- `site:getautoclarity.com`: existing public pages surfaced;
- sampled neutral/local PPI queries: no AutoClarity result observed;
- sampled neutral app-symptom query: no AutoClarity result observed;
- Google Maps/Business Profile ownership: unknown;
- AI citation/referral: none observed;
- important new `llms.txt` and service-catalog URLs: not live, so not indexable;
- similarly named companies/products exist in other countries/categories, which
  makes exact brand/location/entity consistency especially important.

This is directional evidence, not a rank guarantee or an exhaustive duplicate
search. Repeat the small query set after a safe deployment and then weekly until
initial indexing stabilizes; do not manufacture searches or engagement.
