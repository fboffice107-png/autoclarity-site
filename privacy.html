Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

AutoClarity is an informational vehicle-guidance application designed to help users better understand possible vehicle issues using structured diagnostic logic inspired by real-world automotive troubleshooting practices.

This Privacy Policy covers the AutoClarity iPhone app. It describes the currently available version 2.0.1 separately from planned cloud report features for version 2.1.0. Those planned features are not available in version 2.0.1; the descriptions below apply only if they are released and enabled in a later version you use.

1. Information We Collect

Diagnostic answers and reports in version 2.0.1. The app processes your symptom selections and generates and saves reports on your device. Its normal diagnostic flow does not upload your full answers or report content to an AutoClarity report server. If you choose to export or share a report, the app or service you select receives that report and handles it under its own policies.

Information collected by the app and its service providers. Firebase Analytics and RevenueCat receive information used for analytics and purchase services, including:

  • App interactions, such as app opens, diagnostic-flow events, paywall views, and purchase or restore actions
  • Purchase history, product identifiers, prices and currencies, subscription and entitlement status
  • App-instance, installation, device, and app-specific customer identifiers that distinguish an installation or customer over time
  • Approximate location derived from your network IP address for analytics, rather than precise GPS location
  • Technical information, such as device model, operating system, app and SDK versions, and purchase-error information

Network services also process IP addresses and request information to deliver and secure their services. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. Apple processes payments; we do not receive your full payment card details.

Planned cloud report features (not active in version 2.0.1)

If cloud report access is enabled in a later version, AutoClarity will use a verified App Store transaction to establish an app-specific account identifier. This does not require you to give AutoClarity your Apple account email or password. That identifier will link your report access and purchase records across devices and will also be used with Firebase Authentication and RevenueCat.

The cloud service will store completed report content, including your diagnostic answers, results, report identifiers and dates, and vehicle details you provide, such as year, make, model, engine, mileage, and fuel type. It will also store purchase-verification records, report-credit and free-report usage, recovery status, and account-deletion requests. This supports access, recovery, purchase fulfillment, and abuse prevention. Firebase Authentication, App Check, and backend services will process authentication, app/device verification, and technical request data to protect that access.

2. Analytics and Tracking

We use Firebase Analytics (a Google service) to understand how users interact with the app, including events such as app opens, diagnostic flows, paywall views, and purchases.

This information is used to understand usage and purchases and improve the app and its purchase flow. Analytics records can be linked to a particular app installation or device, even when we do not know your name. Technical information also helps providers understand the use and operation of their SDKs.

The app sends usage and purchase events to Firebase Analytics. It does not send full diagnostic answers or report content as Firebase Analytics event parameters.

3. Purchases and Subscriptions

In version 2.0.1, AutoClarity provides one successfully completed diagnostic report at no charge. Additional new reports are available through an auto-renewing annual subscription. Apple determines introductory-offer eligibility and displays the price that applies before purchase. During the August 15 through December 31, 2026 introductory-offer period, eligible U.S. customers may pay $9.99 upfront for the first year; the subscription then renews at $29.99 per year unless canceled. Localized equivalents apply outside the United States, and customers who are not eligible for the introductory offer see the current localized standard annual price. The introductory offer is not a free trial, lifetime purchase, or permanent renewal price.

  • All payments are processed securely by Apple via the App Store
  • We do not store or process your payment card details
  • Subscriptions are managed or canceled through your Apple subscription settings
  • Restore Purchases asks Apple and RevenueCat to recheck existing purchase and entitlement records

We use RevenueCat to manage purchase validation, subscription status, and entitlements. RevenueCat processes limited technical and transaction information (such as an app-specific customer identifier, product identifiers, purchase history, and entitlement status) on our behalf as a service provider.

Existing monthly subscribers continue to be validated under their existing subscription while it remains active. Records needed to honor legitimate legacy single-report purchases are also retained and validated so the purchased fulfillment is not removed. AutoClarity 2.0.1 does not offer a new single-report purchase.

If one-report credits become available in a later version, purchase and fulfillment records will also be used to verify those credits and the reports they unlock. The App Store purchase screen will show the available product and applicable price before you buy.

4. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide app functionality and diagnostic reports
  • Verify purchases and subscriptions
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Analyze usage trends
  • Respond to support requests
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

5. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We use the following providers to operate and improve the app:

  • Google/Firebase for app analytics and the related technical information described above
  • RevenueCat for purchase validation, subscription and entitlement management, and purchase analytics
  • For planned cloud features, Firebase authentication and app-verification services and Google Cloud/Firestore backend services for account, report, and purchase-access records

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect users, property, or safety.

Provider information is available in Firebase's privacy and security information, RevenueCat's Privacy Policy, and Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

Report history in version 2.0.1 is stored on your device. Copies you export, share, or include in a device backup may remain separately. Removing the app or local reports does not delete analytics or purchase records already held by service providers.

Retention of analytics, purchase, technical, and support records depends on the type of record, its purpose, and the applicable service settings and provider policies. Purchase records may remain necessary to validate access, handle refunds or disputes, and meet legal obligations.

For planned cloud features, account and report records will be held to provide access and recovery until deleted through the account-deletion process. Limited records of fulfilled purchases, free-report usage, and deletion handling may remain where necessary to prevent duplicate fulfillment, resolve disputes, or comply with law. Provider logs and backups may have separate retention periods.

7. Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of data associated with your use of AutoClarity by emailing support@getautoclarity.com with your app version and details that help us locate the records concerned. Do not send your Apple account password or payment card details. Upon a verified request, we will delete or de-identify the information we and our service providers (such as RevenueCat and Firebase) hold about your app usage, where deletion is technically feasible and not required to be retained by law.

Please note that purchase and billing records are held by Apple and are subject to Apple's own policies; we cannot delete records held by Apple.

If the planned cloud account features become available, the app's Account & Privacy screen will also let you submit and check a deletion request. A received or pending request is not confirmation that deletion has finished. Unused paid credits and unresolved purchases require review before erasure so paid value is not silently discarded. Contact support to check progress.

Deleting app data or a cloud account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel your subscription separately through Apple to stop future renewal charges. Copies you previously exported or shared may remain with their recipients.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the European Union, United Kingdom, or California), you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@getautoclarity.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. You will not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect data. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

AutoClarity is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Informational Use Disclaimer

AutoClarity provides informational guidance only. Outputs are generated based on user input and predefined diagnostic logic and may be incomplete, inaccurate, or not applicable to your specific vehicle.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of changes.

13. Contact

For questions, contact: support@getautoclarity.com
